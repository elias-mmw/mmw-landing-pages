But what if they end up loving music?

Short lessons, play-along music, rewards, and visible progress help kids feel successful. When music feels fun and rewarding, they want to keep going.

But you don’t have to know before you start. Seven free days let you see how they respond before spending anything. After that, My Music Workshop is a much smaller financial commitment than many other ways your child could learn music.

There’s a little risk in finding out, but a lot they could gain.

What kind of instrument do we need?

⌄

Whatever you have is enough to get started.

A piano, digital piano, or basic keyboard will work just fine. Weighted keys give kids the best playing experience, but they can start learning without them.

Begin with what’s available, and you can always think about upgrading later.

↗

Will it work for my child’s level?

⌄

Whether they’re brand new or already playing, there’s a place to begin.

New players can start with the basics, while children with some experience can continue building through reading music, two-hand playing, scales, chords, and chord progressions.

They can begin where it makes sense and keep progressing from there.
